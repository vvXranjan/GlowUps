    How it works

    Nothing new to install for your customers. They message your salon's WhatsApp number like they already do.

    1. Customer messages your salon

      "Haircut kal 5 baje Amit ke saath?" GlowUps understands the service, day, time and stylist.

    2. It checks real free slots

      It offers only times when that stylist is actually free, outside lunch, then asks the customer to confirm.

    3. Your team sees it instantly

      The booking appears on the chair board. Reminders go out on their own. Staff mark it done with one message.

    4. You get the numbers

      Daily report on WhatsApp: customers, earnings, no-shows, top stylist, and who to win back.

    What the first version does
